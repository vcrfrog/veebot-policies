VeeBot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VeeBot, a Discord application providing collectible card and collection-related features, may process information when people use the application.

VeeBot is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord or Karuta.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and how the application is operated, VeeBot may process the following categories of data:

  • Discord identifiers: User IDs and, where needed, server IDs and other identifiers provided through Discord's API.
  • Collection data: Cards claimed, print numbers, card quality, rarity, inventory, favourites, wishlists and collection progress.
  • Game data: Virtual currency, rewards, packs, upgrades, frames, settings and other in-bot progress.
  • Command data: Information necessary to process commands, buttons and other interactions.
  • Technical information: Error reports and diagnostic information needed to operate, troubleshoot and protect the application.

VeeBot is designed to use Discord's interaction and application interfaces for its features. It is not intended to collect Discord passwords or authentication tokens from users.

The bot's actual data collection depends on the features implemented and permissions granted to the application.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Process commands and respond to Discord interactions.
  • Maintain card collections and assign unique print numbers.
  • Manage wishlists, rarity, quality, upgrades and virtual items.
  • Save progress and restore it when the bot restarts.
  • Diagnose errors, prevent abuse and maintain the application.
  • Respond to requests concerning personal information.

4. Storage and Security

Depending on the deployment, information may be stored in a local database or on infrastructure used to run the bot. The intended initial implementation uses a database to maintain collections and game progress.

Reasonable measures will be taken to protect stored information and restrict access. However, no storage system or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Bot credentials and authentication secrets should be kept confidential and must not be included in public source code or public website files.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

VeeBot does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by the following services where necessary for the application to operate:

  • Discord: Discord processes account and interaction information under its own policies when you use Discord and interact with the bot.
  • Hosting providers: If the bot is hosted using an external service, that provider may process technical information necessary to deliver its service.
  • Website hosting: The policy website may be hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security purposes.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the security of the application and its users.

6. Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the bot's features, preserve collection progress, resolve disputes, maintain security or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the application's configuration. Diagnostic records may be kept separately from collection data.

7. Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their use of VeeBot by contacting the application developer through the contact method published on the application's official profile or website.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law. Some information may need to be retained where there is a lawful reason to do so.

Deleting data from VeeBot does not automatically delete information held by Discord or other independent services. Such information is subject to those services' own policies and procedures.

8. Children's Privacy

VeeBot is not intended to bypass Discord's minimum age requirements or any applicable legal requirements for children's privacy. Users must comply with the age requirements and policies applicable to their use of Discord.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates when the current version took effect.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to request deletion of information, contact the application developer through the contact method published on the application's official profile or website.
